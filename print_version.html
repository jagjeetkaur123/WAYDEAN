About Waydean Nursery

Established in 2010 by Dean Morris with Wayne and Wendy Protheroe, Waydean Nursery specialises in high-quality citrus production for growers across Australia. Today the nursery operates six insect-proof hothouses with a capacity of 50,000 trees.

We welcome nursery inspections by appointment and offer a wide range of citrus varieties on rootstocks selected to suit your conditions.

What We Offer

All Citrus Varieties

Public and private varieties available, including lemons, oranges, mandarins, limes, finger limes and grapefruit.

Custom Rootstock Options

All rootstocks are grown from seed on-site and matched with varieties to suit your soil, climate and growing goals.

Wholesale Supply

Reliable supply for commercial planting programs, farm developments and specialist growers.

Quality & Nursery Systems

Insect-proof Hothouses

Production takes place under strict insect control to preserve tree health and quality.

Virus-tested Budwood

All budwood is tested and selected for clean propagation and long-term performance.

Sterile Growth Medium

Trees are grown in 4L bags using sterile potting mix to ensure strong establishment.

Strict Quality Control

Every tree and rootstock is monitored under rigorous quality systems before dispatch.
